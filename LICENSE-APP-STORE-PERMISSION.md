## Why this file exists

Bogner Chess is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or
later. The app is distributed through Apple's App Store, and the App Store's
terms put usage rules and DRM on every download. Whether those terms can be
reconciled with sections 6 and 10 of the GPL is disputed, and the cautious
reading is that they cannot. A copyright holder can settle the question for
their own code by granting an *additional permission* under section 7 of the
GPL, which is what this file will do.

## What the permission will cover

- Only files written for this app. They are recognisable by the header
  `SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-or-later` together with a reference to this
  file.
- Conveying the app in object form through an app store whose terms would
  otherwise conflict with the GPL, provided the complete corresponding source
  of that exact build is publicly available under the GPL.

## What it will not cover

- Files adapted from `lichess-org/mobile` or `lichess-org/flutter-chessground`.
  Those stay under GPL-3.0 as their authors published them. Only their
  copyright holders could add a permission, so this file cannot. They are
  listed in `NOTICE`.
- Third-party packages and assets, which keep their own licences.
- Anything beyond distribution. The permission will not weaken copyleft: a
  modified version must still be released under the GPL, and anyone may remove
  the permission from their own copy, as section 7 allows.
